CONTENT CORRECTIONS (the original data source was fixed, not the UI)
--------------------------------------------------------------------
- lessons/basics/lesson-002.js — 30 exercises ("Subject Pronouns",
  listen-and-type) had Arabic adjectives inside the ENGLISH sentence
  and acceptedAnswers fields. Examples of what was fixed:
      "I am متعب."   → "I am tired."
      "She is سعيدة." → "She is happy."
      "They are سعداء." → "They are happy."   (60 string fixes total:
      30 en + 30 acceptedAnswers). Arabic adjectives are invariant in
      English: She is tired. / They are tired.
  The `ar` fields (أنا متعب. / هي متعبة.) are intentional Arabic
  translations and were left untouched, as were Arabic `hint` fields
  elsewhere (they render with dir:auto as learning aids).
- scripts/content/gen-basics.js — ROOT CAUSE fixed: the "{S} {be} {ADJ}."
  pattern filled the `en` slot with ADJ_G[a].m (Arabic masculine form).
  Now `en: a` (the English adjective). Any future regeneration keeps
  the course pure English.
- Verified with scripts/validate-lessons.js (PASSED, 123 lessons ↔
  123 manifest entries) and the full test suite (8/8).
- .github/workflows/deploy.yml trigger checked — it correctly fires on
  `branches: [main]` (no change needed).
